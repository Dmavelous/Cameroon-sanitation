What We Do

Biodigester Modern Sanitation Services

Each service card uses the same reusable class, which keeps the design consistent.

01

Site assessment & feasibility study

We evaluate your location, available space, waste/water flow, and conditions to confirm the biodigester system will work and to size it correctly.

02

Design & engineering of the biodigester system

We plan how waste will be collected, how the biodigester will be built, and how inlet/outlet connections will be made so the system performs reliably.

03

Installation & system integration

We construct and connect the biodigester to the toilets/waste sources, ensuring proper plumbing, sealing, and correct routing for safe sanitation.

04

Commissioning & performance testing

After installation, we test and start the system to verify it is working properly—checking connections and ensuring it’s ready for day-to-day use.

05

Operation & maintenance (O&M) support

We provide routine checks, troubleshooting, and repairs so the biodigester modern sanitation continues to operate efficiently and prevents breakdowns.
